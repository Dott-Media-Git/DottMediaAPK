## Text for App Review notes and reply

Dotti AI is a business productivity app for small-business owners, marketing teams, and social-media managers. It helps them create, organize, schedule, and analyze social-media content for the accounts they control. The app is intended for adult business users and functions consistently in all App Store regions.

Review access: use the existing demo account entered in App Review Information. It has an active plan and sample business data. From the dashboard, open Create Content to generate a draft, choose a supported destination, preview it, and save or schedule it. Open Profile for account and billing information. Account deletion is available in Profile > Account > Delete account permanently; the confirmation explains that the Dotti profile, connected social credentials, CRM data, content, automations, and account access are deleted.

Dotti AI uses Firebase Authentication and Firebase data services for identity and app data, Dott Media's hosted API for content, scheduling, and analytics, and connected third-party social platforms only when the reviewer chooses to connect an account. Supported connections include Meta/Facebook/Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, X, WhatsApp, and Google where applicable. No third-party account is needed to view the demo workspace.

The attached physical-device recording demonstrates launch, sign-in, the main content workflow, account controls, and account-deletion flow. It does not use simulated content or a simulator recording.
